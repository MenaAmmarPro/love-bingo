A few little rules

  • Tap a square when it happens to you two. The middle is always free — that one is us.
  • Five in a row — across, down or corner to corner — is a bingo.
  • Edit squares swaps every square for a text box, so you can write your own moments. Changes save as you type.
  • New card shuffles the squares you wrote and keeps them; Clear marks only wipes the ticks.
  • Save as picture makes a shareable image of your finished card.
